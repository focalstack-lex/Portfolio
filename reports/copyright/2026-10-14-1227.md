# Copyright and Legal Compliance Audit: Tote page

- Project: Tote product page and download (part of the Lex Matondo portfolio)
- Date: 2026-10-14 12:27 (Asia/Manila)
- Mode: full (source, live headers, live behavior)
- Stack: static HTML and CSS on Vercel, no JavaScript; the download is a Tauri 2 Windows installer (Rust, React)
- Target root: `C:\Users\User\Documents\Portfolio_Lex\tote` and `vercel.json`; live at https://www.codewithlex.site/tote
- Out of scope: the rest of the portfolio

Legal framing: Tote is free software for Philippine public school teachers, so the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) is the most relevant privacy law; GDPR or CCPA would matter only for users in the EU or California. Findings marked "technical" are objective; findings marked "practice" are the safe course rather than a cited statutory breach. This is not legal advice.

## Lens 1: Intellectual Property and Trademark

**[P2] The installer ships open source code and a font without their license notices** (technical)
- Evidence: the Tote app bundles React and lucide-react (`package.json:27-31`, MIT and ISC), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (`src/main.tsx:3-4`, SIL OFL 1.1), Tauri and many Rust crates (`src-tauri/Cargo.toml`, MIT or Apache-2.0). Neither the repo nor the bundle has a third-party notices file; `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json` bundles no license resource.
- Risk: MIT and ISC require the copyright and permission notice in "all copies or substantial portions"; OFL requires the license to travel with the font. Distributing the binary without them is a license-condition breach, low enforcement risk but real.
- Remediation: generate `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` (for example with `cargo about` for Rust and `license-checker` for npm), ship it in the install folder, and link it from the page.

**[P2] Lucide icons on the page are served without the ISC notice** (technical)
- Evidence: `tote/assets/icons/*.svg` (19 files, exported from the design; they are Lucide icons, the same set the app uses through `lucide-react`). No license file in `tote/assets/icons/`.
- Remediation: add `tote/assets/icons/LICENSE.txt` with the Lucide ISC license (and Feather MIT notice it carries).

**[P3] Third-party product names are used descriptively, with no affiliation claims** (compliant, optional polish)
- Evidence: `tote/index.html:137` (Messenger, Gmail, DepEd), `:142` (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), `:12` and `:51` (Windows). No logos, no "official" or partner wording.
- Remediation (optional): one line on a license page: "Windows, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Gmail and Messenger are trademarks of their owners. Tote is not affiliated with them or with DepEd."

Compliant: the Tote logo is Lex's own; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next is self-hosted with its license (`tote/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`); no hotlinked images; mock file names and the student name are fictional.

## Lens 2: Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

**[P2] Small captions on the page background fall below 4.5:1 contrast** (technical, WCAG 1.4.3)
- Evidence: `.caption` uses `--text-faint` #6b7872 (`tote/tote.css`, `.caption` rule) at 13px. On the canvas #eef2ee that is 4.08:1: `tote/index.html:51` (hero "Free. Windows 10 and 11...") and `:239` (footer). The same color on white (install card captions) is 4.61:1 and passes.
- Remediation: use `--text-muted` #4f5c55 for captions on the canvas (6.20:1).

Compliant (checked): `lang="en"` (`:2`); every decorative image has `alt=""` and the two product mocks are `role="img"` with descriptive labels (`:55`, `:154`); skip link; visible focus ring (5.70:1 against the canvas); every link and button at least 40 px (Playwright, 320 to 1440 px); no horizontal scroll at 320 px (reflow, 1.4.10); reduced motion respected; heading order h1, h2, h3; other text pairs 4.69:1 to 9.01:1.

## Lens 3: Data Privacy, Cybersecurity and Cookies

**[P1] No privacy notice, although the app now contacts the site** (practice, RA 10173 transparency)
- Evidence: the nav "Privacy" link (`tote/index.html:33`) goes to a feature section (`:184-193`), not a notice. Since 0.2.0 every installed Tote requests `https://www.codewithlex.site/tote/latest.json` up to once a day (`src-tauri/src/update.rs`), sending its IP address and the user agent `tauri-plugin-updater/2.13.3`; Vercel, the host, records request metadata.
- Risk: an IP address can identify a person, so this is processing of personal information; the law expects people to be told what is collected, why, and by whom. The page's own promises are accurate, but they are not a notice.
- Remediation: a short `/tote/privacy` page: what stays on the laptop (everything), what the update check sends (IP address and app name, to Vercel, once a day), why (to offer new versions), how to turn it off (Settings), and a contact.

**[P1] No license or terms for the downloadable software** (practice)
- Evidence: no license, terms or "as is" wording on `tote/index.html`; the MIT license exists only in the Tote repo (`LICENSE:1-3`); the installer shows none.
- Risk: users get no statement of what they may do with the software or of the warranty disclaimer.
- Remediation: footer link "MIT License" to `/tote/license` (license text, warranty disclaimer, third-party notices, trademark line).

Compliant: no cookies (no `Set-Cookie` on `/tote` or `/tote/latest.json`), no analytics or tracking scripts, no forms, no third-party requests (page loads only same-origin files); HTTPS with HSTS, a strict CSP (`script-src 'none'`), `nosniff`, frame protection (`vercel.json`).

## Lens 4: Consumer Protection and Accurate Descriptions

**[P2] "Nothing moves until you say so" contradicts automatic filing** (practice, accuracy)
- Evidence: heading `tote/index.html:186`; the feature card at `:137` says every finished download is moved into School Files automatically.
- Remediation: "Old files stay put until you say so." (the paragraph under it already says old files).

**[P3] "Reads inside ... PDF files" does not mention scanned PDFs** (practice)
- Evidence: `tote/index.html:142` and the meta description (`:7`); Tote extracts text only, so scanned pages and photos are found by name, not content (OCR is on the v1.1 list).
- Remediation: "It reads inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text PDFs."

**[P3] "Windows 10 and 11" is verified on Windows 11 only** (practice)
- Evidence: `tote/index.html:12`, `:51`; every native check ran on Windows 11 (this machine). Tauri and WebView2 support Windows 10, but Tote itself has not been run there.
- Remediation: test once on Windows 10, or say "Windows 11 (Windows 10 should work)".

Compliant: free with no hidden charges, no subscriptions, no reviews or testimonials (none fabricated), the unsigned installer is disclosed with the SmartScreen steps and a SHA-256.

## Lens 5: Product Liability and Warranty

**[P2] No "as is" warranty disclaimer for software that moves files** (practice)
- Evidence: Tote moves downloads automatically and offers Recycle Bin and Organize; the page states no warranty terms (same gap as the Lens 3 license finding).
- Remediation: covered by the license page (MIT already disclaims warranty); keep the existing Undo and Recycle Bin promises, which are accurate.
